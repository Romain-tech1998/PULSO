## Connection mechanisms and constraints

The pilot researches official calendar and event pages first. No JSON-LD, RSS, ICS, API, static/dynamic rendering, or ticketing-platform claim is treated as verified until a dated page-level review records evidence. Public Instagram posts, Stories, and Reels are discovery candidates only; Stories can be inaccessible or ephemeral. Official sources and ticketing links have higher confirmation authority than curators.
